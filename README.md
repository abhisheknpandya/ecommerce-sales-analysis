# E-commerce Sales Analysis

Commercial analytics project exploring sales performance, customer purchasing trends, and profitability through data-driven insights.

## Overview
In this case study, I explore an e-commerce sales dataset to understand how different aspects of a business contribute to overall performance. By analysing sales, profitability and customer purchasing patterns, the aim was to move beyond reporting numbers and instead uncover the story behind the business.

The analysis combines Excel, pivot tables and an interactive dashboard to highlight trends, identify opportunities and communicate insights in a clear and meaningful way.

## Dataset
This project is based on an e-commerce sales dataset containing transactional information such as products, customers, sales, profit and order details. Bringing these elements together made it possible to explore performance from different perspectives rather than focusing on a single metric.

Rather than using the dataset to produce descriptive statistics alone, it provided an opportunity to investigate how different areas of the business were performing, identify patterns in customer purchasing behaviour and explore the factors influencing sales and profitability.

## Approach
Before building the dashboard, I spent time exploring the dataset to understand its structure and the relationships between the different variables. Before leaning into visualisations, I wanted to understand what questions the data could answer and which measures would provide the clearest picture of business performance.

I approached the analysis by breaking the dataset down into different areas of the business, including sales, profitability, customers, products and regional performance. Looking at each area individually made it easier to identify trends, compare performance and recognise patterns that may have been overlooked when viewing the data as a whole.

Excel's PivotTables and PivotCharts were used throughout the analysis to summarise the data, compare results and explore different perspectives. These insights were then brought together into an interactive dashboard, allowing key metrics and trends to be presented in a way that was clear, accessible and easy to interpret.

Throughout the project, my focus was not simply to create charts, but to understand what the analysis revealed and communicate those findings in a way that was clear and accessible. I wanted the dashboard to present information that could be understood by a wider audience, from business stakeholders to individuals with little prior knowledge of the dataset. To me, effective analysis is not only about identifying insights, but also about communicating them in a way that supports understanding and informed discussion.

## Dashboard
The dashboard was designed to bring together the key findings from the analysis into a single interactive view. It provides an overview of sales performance, profitability and customer activity, allowing trends and comparisons to be explored quickly through a combination of KPIs, charts and slicers.

Rather than presenting every metric available, the dashboard focuses on the measures that provide the clearest picture of business performance, making the analysis easier to interpret and navigate.
